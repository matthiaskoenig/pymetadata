# Release notes for pymetadata 0.6.5
![pymetadata](./docs/images/favicon/pymetadata-100x100-300dpi.png)

## Fixes
- Bioregistry URLs now expose their collection and compact term, allowing `RDFAnnotationData` to resolve metadata through OLS while preserving the original URL. Configured OLS ontologies no longer require an identifiers.org namespace, and DRON, MMO and SCDO are now included (#87).
- OLS identifier prefixes are stripped once, case-insensitively, with either a colon or underscore separator. Identifiers such as `SIO_001013`, `OBI_0000070` and `chebi:37924` now produce the correct term IRI (#87).
