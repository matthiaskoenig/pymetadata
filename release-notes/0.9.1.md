# Release notes for pymetadata 0.9.1
![pymetadata](./docs/images/favicon/pymetadata-100x100-300dpi.png)

## Fixes

- A term of `pymetadata.ontologies` is a resource of an annotation, `RDFAnnotation(BQB.IS, PBPKO.MOLECULE)`, and is written as its `url` (#119). The term was taken as its identifier, `COB_0000013`, which names no collection: pymetadata logged "Resource `COB_0000013` could not be split in collection and term" and the bare identifier ended up as the `rdf:resource` of the annotation, e.g. in the SBML models of pkdb_models which annotate their species with `PBPKO.MOLECULE`.
- `OntologyTerm.url` is the url which resolves the term (#119): the identifiers.org url if the identifiers.org registry has the collection of the term and the term matches its pattern, otherwise the bioregistry.io url. It was always `https://identifiers.org/<curie>`, which identifiers.org refuses for PBPKO, KISAO, COB, BFO and NCBITaxon (not registered under that prefix) and for OBI and RO (the prefix is not part of the term there, `https://identifiers.org/obi:OBI_0000014`). Examples: `SBO.SIMPLE_CHEMICAL.url` stays `https://identifiers.org/SBO:0000247`, `PBPKO.PHYSIOLOGICALLY_BASED_PHARMACOKINETIC_MODEL.url` is `https://bioregistry.io/PBPKO:00003`, `KISAO.CVODE.url` is `https://bioregistry.io/KISAO:0000019`. The url is taken from the registry, which `RDFAnnotation` loads anyway, so the first `url` of a session loads the cached identifiers.org registry.

## Ontologies

- The ontology modules are regenerated from the current releases of SBO, KISAO and PBPKO; nothing changed.
