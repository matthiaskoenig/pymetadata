# Release notes for pymetadata 0.8.0
![pymetadata](./docs/images/favicon/pymetadata-100x100-300dpi.png)

## New

- `UniprotQuery.query` reads the protein information of a UniProt accession (name, organism, genes, length, function), including isoform accessions such as `P69905-2`. Responses are cached, an outdated cache is used when UniProt cannot be reached.
- `ChebiQuery.structure` returns the structure of a ChEBI compound as svg, cached and written atomically.
- `RDFAnnotationData` carries the `ontology`, `iri` and `ols_url` of the term in OLS, and reports the `providers` of a collection with the primary one first, the `collection_name` and `collection_homepage`, and `pattern_match`, whether the term matches the pattern of its collection.
- `OLSQuery` reports the `ontology`, the `iri` and the page of the term in OLS (`ols_url`).
- `WebserviceNotFoundError`, a subclass of `WebserviceError` for a 404 answer, and `get_bytes` for binary downloads.

## Changes

- A collection which is not on OLS gives no information and no warning or error.
- A term which OLS does not know is a warning and no longer an error; an outdated cache entry is not used for it.
- The `url` of an annotation is the url of the official non deprecated provider of the registry, e.g., UniProt links UniProt instead of NCBI Protein.
- A corrupt cache file of the ChEBI and UniProt queries is queried again.
